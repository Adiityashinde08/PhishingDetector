🛡 Understanding Phishing URL Anatomy & Vectors

Subdomain Deception

Attackers place trusted brand names into the subdomain (e.g. paypal.com.verify.attacker.test). Remember that browsers route to the domain right before the TLD!

Raw IP Hostnames

Legitimate websites register domain names. Attackers use numeric IP addresses (e.g. http://192.168.1.1) to evade domain reputation blocklists.

Userinfo '@' Spoofing

Per RFC 3986, characters preceding '@' are treated as credentials. Browsers ignore everything before '@' and route straight to the host after it.

Homograph / Punycode

IDN homograph attacks substitute Latin characters with visually identical Cyrillic or Greek glyphs, producing xn-- Punycode strings.
